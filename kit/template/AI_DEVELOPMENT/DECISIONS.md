# Decisions

One entry per accepted decision, newest first. A superseded decision is never deleted — its
reason is the only record of why the alternative was rejected, and deleting it is how the
same rejected option gets re-proposed a month later.

Keep the entry short. The evidence lives where the evidence lives; link it.

```
## D-0001 — <one-line decision>

- Status: accepted | superseded by D-00NN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Context: what forced a choice
- Decision: what was chosen
- Rejected: what was not chosen, and the specific reason
- Evidence: path, command, or measurement that supports it
- Affects: plan nodes, tasks, criteria, files
```
