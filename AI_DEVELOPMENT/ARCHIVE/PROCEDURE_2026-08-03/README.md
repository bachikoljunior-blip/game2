# Retired procedure documents — 2026-08-03

Removed from force by user instruction. Kept as the origin record for decisions made
under them; **not** in force, and finding a rule here is not grounds to reinstate it.

| File | What it was |
|---|---|
| `PROJECT_OPERATING_PROTOCOL.md` | twelve sections: authority, sources of truth, resume, sessions, planning, integration, measurement, gates, review, dependencies, external actions, completion |
| `ROUND.md` | the executable procedure for one art-direction round — build, capture, critique, dispatch, verify |
| `IPHONE_SE3_ROUND.md` | the same shape for a phone round |

What governs now is the block at the head of `CLAUDE.md`: the goal, how elements and their
references are derived, how a blind comparison decides an element, and what a unit of work is.
**Everything else about method is the worker's own.**

`ROUND.md` is the one worth reading before reinventing anything. Its subject — build,
photograph, judge blind against a reference, repair, re-photograph — is the same loop the new
rules describe, and it carries what went wrong doing it: a staleness trap in its own header, a
review set mixed across two builds, five rounds judged at the wrong quality tier. **Those are
findings, not procedure.** `HANDOFF.md` and `AI_DEVELOPMENT/FAILURES.md` keep them in force.

What did **not** go with these files: the measurement discipline in `CLAUDE.md`, the binding
architecture and ownership contract in `ARCHITECTURE.md`, the constraints in
`AI_DEVELOPMENT/CONSTRAINTS.md`, and the checks behind `npm run check`. Those are either
learned here or machine-enforced, and neither is procedure.
