# M.8 — Verified reusable recipe memory

**TRIGGER**
A successful implementation, diagnosis, repair, test, migration, optimization or recovery
method is likely to recur.

**CONTENT**
Store a recipe only after supporting verification exists. Prefer executable scripts and tested
utilities over long prose.

A useful recipe contains purpose, applicability, non-applicability, required inputs and
dependencies, exact usage, verification method, limitations, known failure modes, and last
verified project revision.

Before reuse, confirm that assumptions, interfaces, versions and dependencies still match.

Record dangerous or repeatedly failed patterns when preserving that knowledge will prevent
recurrence.

Do not turn one-off solutions into permanent abstractions without evidence of reuse value.

**STOP CONDITION**
Deactivate when the method is captured and verified, or when it stops recurring.
